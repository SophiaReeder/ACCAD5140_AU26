Hey There!

Sophia Reeder earned her BA in Communication Technology and minors in Game Studies and Media Production & Analysis from The Ohio State University in 2024. She has worked as an undergraduate research assistant for Chronos Laboratories, where she helped run participants through studies exploring the impacts of games. She was also the social media manager for Ohio State's Engineering Career Services, where she created educational and engaging online content.

She is currently the motion capture GRA and works with both markered and markerless technology. Her research look into how scaffolding the presentation of educational content in serious games can aid player's knowledge retention and transfer of information. She brings her background in communication technology and experience as a UI/UX designer to create intuitive interactive experiences. Sophia hopes to produce diverse and engaging stories that make a difference beyond the digital world. Sophia enjoys creating UI for games, editing motion capture footage, and running user testing.
