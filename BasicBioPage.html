RESEARCH FOCUS
Exploring the genre of mystery in digital serious educational games
and how scaffolding information within these interactive experiences
can contribute to better learning.
ABOUT

Sophia Reeder earned her BA in Communication Technology and minors in Game Studies and Media Production & Analysis from The Ohio State University in 2024. She has worked as an undergraduate research assistant for Chronos Laboratories, where she helped run participants through studies exploring the impacts of games. She was also the social media manager for Ohio State's Engineering Career Services, where she created educational and engaging online content.

She is currently the motion capture GRA and works with both markered and markerless technology. She is currently researching retention, using serious games as an educational tool that promote learning by doing. Sophia enjoys creating UI for games, editing motion capture footage, and running user testing.

Sophia can most frequently be found working in Sullivant Hall and the Motion Lab.
