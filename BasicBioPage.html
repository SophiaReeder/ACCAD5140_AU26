Sophia Reeder

Design MFA, Digital Animation [&] Interactive Media
Research Focus
Exploring how serious educational games can be designed to encourage knowledge retention.

Sophia Reeder is a designer obsessed with UI micro interactions, cats who sit on windows, and games with branching narratives. Sophia has always been fascinated with how people interact with technology and is passionate about producing diverse stories. She is currently pursing an MFA in Design where her research explores how immersive UI for interactive experiences can be inclusively designed. Currently, Sophia is editing movement as a Motion Capture GRA and volunteering her time to help create a medical app for The James: Cancer Treatment and Research Center.
